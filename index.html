About this album

For many of us, the pandemic was a hard time.

It felt like something was taken away from humanity — something we haven't quite gotten back yet.

For me, one way of trying to process it was to make music. I started making songs every day. Therapy songs.

Some are funny.

Some are sad.

Some are hopeful.

Some are angry.

Some are weird.

Some are probably just bad. 😸

But I made them anyway.

This album is basically what came out of that process.

WARMLINES

One thing that helped me a lot while I was making these songs was talking to people on warmlines.

If you've never heard of them, they're peer-support phone lines where you can talk with someone when you need to talk — even if you're not in an immediate crisis.

Many are free and confidential, and they're often staffed by people with their own lived experience.

I didn't know they existed for a long time.

I'm really glad I found them.

Maybe they'll help you, too.
